How the scale works

Each question has its own scale. Each pin shows a reading of a specific passage, not a verdict on a thinker’s entire work. Color marks the position; it does not measure confidence or trace influence.

    Glows mark nearby scored evidence, not regional beliefs. An outlined pin is unscored. A numbered pin stands for several passages close together; select it to zoom in or list them. Europe and North America supply most of the passages, partly because the atlas relies on texts that are freely readable online.
