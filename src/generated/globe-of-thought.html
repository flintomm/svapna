How the scale works

Each question has its own scale. Each pin shows a reading of a specific passage, not a verdict on a thinker’s entire work. Color marks the position; it does not measure confidence or trace influence.

    Colour on the land blends the positions of nearby passages along the scale; it marks local evidence, not regional beliefs. An outlined pin is unscored. A numbered pin stands for several passages close together; selecting it zooms in or lists them. Europe and North America supply most of the passages, partly because the atlas relies on texts that are freely readable online.
